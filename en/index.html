FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions e-commerce merchants ask me most often before starting a project.

Who is Abdelmonem Lefi?

+

I am an e-commerce SEO consultant and PrestaShop specialist, founder of GENISOFT. I work on technical SEO, performance, and security for online stores, for merchants in France (Grenoble, Paris, Lyon) and internationally.

Why hire a PrestaShop expert?

+

Hiring a PrestaShop expert allows you to design custom modules, optimize loading performance for better mobile Core Web Vitals scores, secure your online store against threats, and maximize conversion rates.

What is SXO optimization?

+

SXO (Search eXperience Optimization) combines natural SEO with user experience (UX) optimization. Its goal is not only to rank pages in search engines like Google, but also to ensure smooth navigation that converts visitors into customers.

What maintenance services do you offer?

+

My maintenance plans include regular security updates to the PrestaShop core, module and theme updates, daily or weekly externalized backups, and responsive technical support for bug fixes and incident resolution.

How to optimize a website for voice search?

+

For voice search, it is essential to use natural and conversational language, integrate structured Q&A (FAQ), implement Schema.org microdata (JSON-LD), and have an extremely fast mobile site, since the majority of voice queries are made on mobile devices or voice assistants.
