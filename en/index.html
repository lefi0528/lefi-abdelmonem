Technical SEO & Speed

Speed & Performance Optimization on PrestaShop 8

Platform: PrestaShop 8.1 Sector: Fashion & Accessories (France) Scope: Performance audit, template optimization & caching
🔍 Initial Problem & Diagnostic

Excessive mobile load times detected (> 4.5s in laboratory benchmark), degraded Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), and Core Web Vitals warnings in Google Search Console negatively affecting user experience.

⚙️ Personal Role & Actions

Direct intervention in child theme: deferred and asynchronous execution of non-critical third-party JS scripts, rebuilt native WebP image delivery pipeline, tuned opcode caching, and purged redundant SQL queries in catalog controllers.

📈 Laboratory Test Results

Lab performance test (emulated mobile profile): synthetic performance score improved from 42 to 94/100 during audit benchmarks. Notable reduction in visual template rendering time.

Essential Methodological Distinction: This score reflects a standardized laboratory audit (emulated fast 4G connection). It must not be conflated with real-world Core Web Vitals field validation (CrUX / Search Console 28-day rolling data), which depends on actual visitor devices, connection quality, and user interactions.

Indexing & Architecture

Resolving Crawl & Redirection Bottlenecks

Platform: PrestaShop 1.7 Sector: Furniture & Home Decor (Large Catalog) Scope: Crawl audit, 301 redirect map & sitemap architecture
🔍 Initial Problem & Diagnostic

Substantial product page de-indexing following a category restructuring: circular 301 redirection chains, conflicting canonical tags, and extensive duplicate URLs generated by layered navigation facets.

⚙️ Personal Role & Actions

Restructured redirection plan to eliminate circular 301 loops, standardized canonical tags pointing consistently to the primary product URL, and applied differentiated faceted navigation handling: targeted noindex directives on crawlable low-value attribute combinations, combined with strategic robots.txt crawl exclusions on infinite filter parameters to safeguard crawl budget. Deployed a segmented XML sitemap hierarchy to facilitate Googlebot discovery of priority product URLs.

📈 Measured Results

Progressive resolution of critical crawl errors reported in Google Search Console and steady restoration of regular Googlebot crawl activity across priority catalog pages.

Interpretation Limits: Crawl frequency and traffic recovery timelines depend on Googlebot scheduling cycles and seasonal market demand for specific product lines. Submitting an XML sitemap assists discovery but does not guarantee automatic indexing by Google.

E-Commerce Security

Emergency Store Decontamination & Hardening

Platform: PrestaShop 1.7 / cPanel Sector: B2B Distribution (Belgium) Scope: Emergency cleanup, vulnerability patching & hardening
🔍 Initial Problem & Diagnostic

Store compromised on a weekend via malicious script injections targeting checkout and payment components, resulting in immediate preventative hosting suspension.

⚙️ Personal Role & Actions

Identified and isolated malicious injected scripts, performed thorough cleansing of CMS files and database records, regenerated all cryptographic keys (_COOKIE_KEY_) alongside administrative and database credentials, and implemented server-level application firewall (WAF) filtering rules.

📈 Measured Results

Store sanitized and returned to production in approximately two hours (total downtime reported by the merchant). Hosting suspension successfully lifted.

Interpretation Limits: Emergency incident response removes the active threat and restores operations. Long-term security requires consistent application of official PrestaShop core updates and third-party module patches.

What is SXO optimization?

+

SXO (Search eXperience Optimization) combines natural SEO with user experience (UX) optimization. Its goal is not only to rank pages in search engines like Google, but also to ensure smooth navigation that converts visitors into customers.

What e-commerce maintenance services are offered?

+

How to optimize a website for voice search?

+

For voice search, it is essential to use natural and conversational language, integrate structured Q&A (FAQ), implement Schema.org microdata (JSON-LD), and have an extremely fast mobile site, since the majority of voice queries are made on mobile devices or voice assistants.
